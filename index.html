Community Guidelines

Keep conversations beneficial. Share useful ideas, questions, experiences and resources.

Be respectful. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks, ridicule, harassment and abusive language are not.

Protect privacy. Do not share another person's private information, screenshots or personal stories without permission.

Avoid spam and self-promotion. Relevant recommendations are welcome; repetitive promotion is not.

Use reporting responsibly. Report content that genuinely needs moderator attention.

NLC Community administrators may remove content or restrict access where necessary to protect the community.
