Community Guidelines

Keep conversations beneficial. Share useful ideas, questions, experiences and resources.

Be respectful. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks, ridicule, harassment and abusive language are not.

Protect privacy. Do not share another person's private information without permission.

Avoid spam and self-promotion. Relevant recommendations are welcome; repetitive promotion is not.

Use reporting responsibly. Report content that genuinely needs moderator attention.

Admin Console

Role permissions

Member: Participate and manage their own content.

Moderator: Review reports and moderate community posts/comments.

Admin: Moderator permissions plus member suspension/restoration and pinned announcements.

Owner: Full access plus assigning or removing team roles. The Owner role is protected.

Moderation approach

Use the least restrictive action appropriate: dismiss harmless reports, remove problematic content, and suspend access only when necessary. Reports are private.

Admin routine

Check the moderation queue regularly, encourage useful discussions, review member access when necessary, and use the Audit Log to understand administrative actions.
